\subsubsection*{I. Area}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Find the area of the region enclosed by \(x=y^4\), \(y=\sqrt{2-x}\), and \(y=0\).
  \item Find the area of the region bounded by \(y=\tan x\) and \(y=2\sin x\) on \(-\tfrac{\pi}{3}\le x\le \tfrac{\pi}{3}\).
  \item Find the number \(b\) such that the line \(y=b\) divides the region bounded by \(y=x^2\) and \(y=4\) into two regions of equal area.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{II. Volume}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The region bounded by \(y=x^2\) and \(x=y^2\) is rotated about \(y=1\). Find the volume of the resulting solid.
  \item The region bounded by \(x=y^2\) and \(x=1-y^2\) is rotated about \(x=3\). Find the volume of the resulting solid. (setup only)
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{III. Work}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item If \(6\,\mathrm{J}\) of work is needed to stretch a spring from \(10\ \mathrm{cm}\) to \(12\ \mathrm{cm}\) and another \(10\,\mathrm{J}\) from \(12\ \mathrm{cm}\) to \(14\ \mathrm{cm}\), find the work required to stretch the spring from \(9.5\ \mathrm{cm}\) to \(13.5\ \mathrm{cm}\).
  \item A bucket of mass \(10\,\mathrm{kg}\) is hoisted from ground level to a platform \(12\,\mathrm{m}\) high at constant speed. The rope passes over a light pulley at the platform and has linear mass density \(0.8\,\mathrm{kg/m}\). Initially the bucket contains \(36\,\mathrm{kg}\) of water that leaks at a constant rate and is completely gone by the time the bucket reaches height \(8\,\mathrm{m}\); after that, no water remains. Compute the total work done by the lifting force.
\end{enumerate}
